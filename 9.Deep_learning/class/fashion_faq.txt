UrbanTrend Fashion Store - Sales & Customer FAQ

Q1: What products are available at UrbanTrend Fashion Store?
A1: UrbanTrend Fashion Store offers men’s, women’s, and kids’ clothing including casual wear, formal wear, ethnic wear, jeans, jackets, t-shirts, dresses, footwear, and fashion accessories.

Q2: Do you offer cash on delivery?
A2: Yes, UrbanTrend Fashion Store provides cash on delivery for selected pin codes across India. Availability may vary based on location.

Q3: What is the return policy?
A3: Customers can return products within 7 days of delivery if the item is unused, unwashed, and in original condition with tags intact.

Q4: How long does delivery take?
A4: Delivery usually takes 3 to 7 working days depending on the customer’s location and product availability.

Q5: Are there any ongoing discounts or sales?
A5: UrbanTrend Fashion Store regularly runs seasonal sales, festive discounts, and special offers which are updated on our website and in-store.

Q6: How can I track my order?
A6: Once the order is shipped, customers receive a tracking link via SMS or email to monitor delivery status.

Q7: What payment methods are accepted?
A7: We accept credit cards, debit cards, UPI, net banking, wallets, and cash on delivery.

Q8: Are your products original and branded?
A8: Yes, all products sold at UrbanTrend Fashion Store are 100% original and sourced directly from authorized manufacturers.

Q9: Can I exchange a product?
A9: Yes, exchanges are allowed within 7 days of delivery for size or color issues, subject to stock availability.

Q10: Do you have a physical store?
A10: Yes, UrbanTrend Fashion Store has physical outlets in major cities as well as an online shopping platform.

Q11: Is there a size guide available?
A11: Yes, a detailed size guide is available on each product page to help customers choose the correct fit.

Q12: What should I do if I receive a damaged product?
A12: If a damaged or defective product is received, customers should contact customer support within 48 hours of delivery for a replacement or refund.

Q13: Do you offer bulk or wholesale purchases?
A13: Yes, bulk and wholesale orders are accepted for businesses and resellers. Customers can contact our sales team for special pricing.

Q14: Are international orders supported?
A14: Currently, UrbanTrend Fashion Store delivers only within India. International shipping will be available soon.

Q15: How can I contact customer support?
A15: Customers can contact us via email, phone, or live chat available on our website during business hours.
