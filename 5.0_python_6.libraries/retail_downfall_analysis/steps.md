Participation in this competition is compulsory for all students.

 What is the Challenge?
Your team has to act as Data Analysts for a company that is currently facing a business downfall.

Choose any company or industry of your choice and use real-world data to analyse its business performance. 

Your team needs to:
1️⃣ Choose a company/industry that is facing a problem or decline.
2️⃣ Find and use relevant real-world data.
3️⃣ Analyse the data using Excel and SQL.
4️⃣ Create a dashboard using Power BI or Tableau.
5️⃣ Find out why the business is going down.
6️⃣ Give practical solutions to improve the business.
7️⃣ Present your findings as professional Data Analysts.


Come in formal dress.
Bring your completed project and analysis.
Present with confidence.
Imagine you are presenting your findings to the management of the company. 
Remember, this is not just a dashboard-making activity. 

Your goal is to find the problem, understand the reason behind it, and provide a data-driven solution. 

Think like a Data Analyst. Work like a Data Analyst. Present like a Data Analyst. 

Looking forward to seeing your ideas and amazing presentations!
