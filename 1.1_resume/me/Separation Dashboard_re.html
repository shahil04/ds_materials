Important Information

Please read carefully before initiating your exit process

Before You Begin
•

You will need to serve a notice period of 45 days

•

All company assets must be returned before your last working day

•

Full & Final settlement will be processed once all departmental clearances are completed and NOCs are received from all concerned departments.

•

Reason is Mandatory when initiating separation

•

Paid leaves cannot be availed during the notice period. Any leave taken during this period will be treated as Leave Without Pay (LWP).

•

The Turnaround Time (TAT) for processing the Full & Final settlement is 45 days from the employee's last working day, subject to completion of all clearances.

Please Note
•

Retention: HR or your manager may propose retention offers during the exit process

•

Revocation: You may revoke your resignation if it has not yet been approved by your Reporting Manager,once the Reporting Manager approves the resignation, it cannot be withdrawn.

•

Final Settlement: Full & Final settlement will be paid according to company policy after all clearances

Before You Proceed, Have You Considered?

We value your contributions and would love to explore ways to address any concerns you may have. Consider speaking with your Manager or HR about growth opportunities, role changes, or any challenges you're facing before making this decision.
